magnet_sorter · demo wiring

One SG90 servo on the Arduino Uno R3

Three jumper wires, no external supply. This matches the firmware already flashed on the board, which drives the base servo from pin D9.

Pin by pin

Servo wireJumperUno pin
orange (signal)orange 1D9
red (+)orange 25V
brown (−)blackGND (next to 5V)

Find D9 from the USB end of the long top header: GND, 13, 12, 11, 10, then 9. On the bottom header, 5V is the fourth hole from the left, right before the two GNDs.

Pins 0 and 1 are the USB serial link. A servo on them stops the laptop from talking to the board.

Then run it

  1. Plug the three jumpers as above, with the board still on USB. The green ON light stays lit.
  2. Tell me the wires are moved. I'll send servo commands over the serial port and the horn sweeps.
  3. If the horn twitches or the board resets, the servo is drawing too much. Move red to the separate 5 V supply and join that supply's GND to an Uno GND.

Full arm later, same firmware: D9 base, D10 shoulder, D11 elbow, D7 relay for the magnet, D6 conveyor servo. Servos then run from the 5 V 5 A supply, never from the Uno's 5V pin.
