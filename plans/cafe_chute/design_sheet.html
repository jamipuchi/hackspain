One chute, one swing gate, two bins

Beans roll one at a time down a cardboard chute with a paper floor. A fixed phone reads each bean as it passes. Good beans roll straight off the end into Sin defecto visible. For a suspect, a 6 cm section of the right wall swings 25° into the channel and steers it out through its own opening into Para revisar. One servo, nothing stops, nobody touches the beans. All dimensions in centimetres.

Materials: 2.5 mm foam board (cartón pluma), one SG90 micro servo, the Arduino Uno R3 already flashed with the magnet_arm firmware, three jumper wires, an iPhone on a small tripod, white printer paper, a toothpick, hot glue or PVA, a bowl and a cup. Roasted arabica beans about 1.0 to 1.3 cm long, 0.7 to 0.9 wide, 0.45 thick. Narrow the channel to 2.2 for green beans.

Door angles

FLUSH
door in line with the wall: S 90 90 75 (base channel = door servo on D9)
OPEN
25° into the channel, free end landing on the soft-stop pad on the far wall: S 65 90 75. Use 115 if it turns the wrong way
SWING
stock firmware ramp: about 0.35 s for 25°. Ask for the fast-door channel (about 400 °/s) to get 0.1 s; the pad takes the impact
DWELL
send OPEN as soon as the verdict is "suspect"; send FLUSH 0.5 s after the bean was due at the door

Fit the horn with the servo at 90 so FLUSH is truly flush. Then check that 65 lets the door's free end touch the far wall without jamming against it.

Camera and run sequence

ZONE
9 to 15 cm from the top. Mark the ends with pencil ticks on the wall tops, outside the image.
HEIGHT
20 cm lens to paper, lens perpendicular to the paper and aligned with the slope. Use 2× if available.
MOTION
the bean crosses the zone at about 20 cm/s. Bright, even light keeps the shutter short; a 1/500 s exposure gives under 0.5 mm of blur.
LIGHT
from the side of the tripod. No lamp behind the phone: its shadow lands on the zone.
FEED
one bean by hand at the top every 2 s. Never two in the zone at once.

Run sequence

  1. Door FLUSH. Drop one bean at the top.
  2. Bean enters the zone about 0.6 s later. The laptop grabs the frame with the bean nearest the zone centre.
  3. Crop, find the dark blob on white paper, measure colour and outline.
  4. Colour in the normal band and outline intact → no visible defect: nothing happens, the bean rolls off the end into the bowl.
  5. Too pale, black patches, broken outline → suspect: OPEN now. The bean meets the angled door at 30 cm and slides out through the opening into the cup.
  6. FLUSH 0.5 s later. Next bean.

Software loop

Reuses the serial class and the firmware from the magnet sorter. The phone reaches the laptop as a webcam (Continuity Camera on macOS). Everything runs locally so the verdict lands well inside the 0.45 s budget.

import cv2, time, numpy as np
from hardware import SerialArduino            # ~/robotics/magnet_sorter

PORT, CAM = "/dev/cu.usbmodem21401", 1        # CAM index of the iPhone
FLUSH, OPEN = "S 90 90 75", "S 65 90 75"      # door servo on D9 (base channel)
ZONE = (x0, y0, x1, y1)                       # pixel box of the 9–15 cm zone, set once
T_ZONE_TO_DOOR = 0.55                         # s, measured with a stopwatch on your slope

door = SerialArduino(PORT); door.write(FLUSH)
cap = cv2.VideoCapture(CAM); cap.set(cv2.CAP_PROP_FPS, 60)

def inspect(frame):
    z = frame[ZONE[1]:ZONE[3], ZONE[0]:ZONE[2]]
    g = cv2.cvtColor(z, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)
    mask = g < 150                            # bean = dark on white paper
    if mask.sum() < 400: return None          # nothing in the zone
    L = cv2.cvtColor(z, cv2.COLOR_BGR2LAB)[..., 0][mask].mean()
    black = (g[mask] < 45).mean()
    return "suspect" if L > 120 or black > 0.35 else "ok"

seen, close_at = False, None
while True:
    ok, frame = cap.read()
    v = inspect(frame)
    if v and not seen:                        # first frame with a bean in the zone
        seen = True
        if v == "suspect":
            door.write(OPEN); close_at = time.time() + T_ZONE_TO_DOOR + 0.5
    elif v is None:
        seen = False                          # zone empty again, ready for the next bean
    if close_at and time.time() > close_at:
        door.write(FLUSH); close_at = None

Why the swing gate, and what it trades away

Compared for beans fed one at a time down this chute. The swing gate is the chosen design: one servo, no stop, the simplest cardboard work. Its cost is timing: the verdict has to arrive before the bean does.
